About Tania

Community first, with the numbers on the table

Tania Savory is a licensed real estate salesperson with HomeSmart Cross Island. Her ties to Freeport and Nassau County go back more than 30 years, so many of the blocks she shows are blocks she already knows well.

Before real estate, she worked as a data analyst. That background guides how she studies pricing and comparable sales with every client. She holds a Master of Science degree along with the ABR and RENE designations.
